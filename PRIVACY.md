# fasttab privacy policy

_Last updated: November 15, 2026_

fasttab does not collect, transmit, sell or share any user data. It makes no network requests.

## What fasttab reads, and why

To search and switch, fasttab reads, inside your browser only:

- the titles and URLs of your open tabs and tab groups,
- your recently closed tabs and windows,
- your bookmarks,
- your browsing history.

## What fasttab stores

fasttab keeps the following in your browser's local extension storage, on your device:

- usage-based ranking scores (how often and how recently you visited a page), so the tabs you use most come first,
- a snapshot of your tab list, so the switcher opens instantly,
- site icons (Firefox and Safari only), so results show favicons.

This data never leaves your device. Removing the extension deletes it.

## Page access

When you press the shortcut, fasttab shows its search overlay on the current page. In Firefox you can optionally allow the shortcut on every site; that adds a small script that only listens for the shortcut key. fasttab does not read or change page content.
