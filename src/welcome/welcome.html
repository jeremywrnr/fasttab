fasttab

Press ⌃K on any page, type a few letters, and hit ↵. ⌃K then ↵ jumps back to your previous tab.

Make ⌃K work everywhere

Some sites keep the shortcut for themselves. In Google Docs, ⌃K deletes to the end of the line, and Figma and online code editors grab keys too. Turn this on and fasttab gets the key first on every site.

Firefox will ask to let fasttab access all websites. fasttab only listens for its shortcut there, and sends nothing anywhere.
